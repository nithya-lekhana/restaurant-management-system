01 / OVERVIEW

Introduction

A database-driven web application designed to organize and simplify daily restaurant operations.

Traditional restaurant management may involve manual reservation records, handwritten orders, separate billing records and time-consuming report preparation.

The project provides a centralized system for managing restaurant information through a web interface connected to a relational database.

Integrated operations

Table reservations · Customer records · Menu management · Food orders · Billing · Payments · Management reports

02 / THE NEED

Problem Statement

Manual restaurant management can lead to:

01 · Reservations

Errors in scheduling and table allocation.

02 · Customer records

Duplicate or inconsistent customer information.

03 · Order tracking

Difficulty tracking food orders and kitchen progress.

04 · Billing

Errors in bill calculations, discounts and taxes.

05 · Reporting

Delays in revenue and performance reports.

06 · Data consistency

Data duplication and difficulty maintaining accurate records.

Proposed solution — A centralized database-driven web application that improves record management, validates operational data and provides structured access to restaurant information.

03 / TARGETS

Project Objectives

01 · Database design

Design a relational database for restaurant operations.

02 · Reservations

Manage customers, tables, dining areas and reservations.

03 · Menu and orders

Organize menu items and food orders.

04 · Kitchen

Track kitchen processing and order status.

05 · Billing

Generate bills with applicable discounts and taxes.

06 · Payments

Record payments and maintain billing status.

07 · Reporting

Generate revenue and restaurant performance reports.

08 · DBMS concepts

Apply normalization, keys, constraints, joins, triggers, views and transactions.
