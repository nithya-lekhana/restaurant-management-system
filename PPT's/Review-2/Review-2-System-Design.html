06 / DATABASE LOGIC

Integrity & Business Rules

Database rules protect the consistency of restaurant records.

Constraints

  • Primary and foreign keys maintain relationships
  • UNIQUE constraints help prevent duplicate customer contacts
  • CHECK constraints restrict invalid values
  • NOT NULL and DEFAULT enforce required fields and default states

Triggers & views

  • Reservation validation helps prevent invalid or overlapping bookings
  • Billing triggers restrict changes to closed bills
  • Discount validation blocks inactive discounts
  • Views can simplify queries, such as finding available tables

These mechanisms apply DBMS integrity rules and business logic at the data layer.
